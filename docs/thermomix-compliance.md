## Standing rules for future edits

- Always write the mark as **Thermomix®** (with the ® symbol) on first and prominent
  uses, including "Thermomix® TM7".
- Never imply the site is operated by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Vorwerk /
  Thermomix USA / Vorwerk LLC.
- No on-site cart, pricing table, or checkout. Purchases must go through the
  official `shop.thermomix.com/consultant/109102` link.
- Keep the footer disclaimer on every page (`index.html`, `404.html`, and any new
  pages).
- Do not state specific incentive dollar amounts unless Thermomix's current
  official program materials use that figure.
- Keep "Independent Thermomix® Consultant" visible near the top of every page and
  in the footer.
