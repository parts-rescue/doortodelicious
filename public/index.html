About Karen

I found joy in cooking again

I started cooking with a Thermomix® while living in Germany, and it gave me back something I'd lost — along with functionality I never knew a kitchen could have. Now I'm excited to bring that to people here in the US.

I've been an Independent Thermomix® Consultant for over three years, first in Germany and now here. FAQ

Common questions

What happens in a cooking demo?

We cook a dish or two together, in your kitchen or over video, so you can see it work, taste the result and ask whatever you like. It's relaxed, hands-on and usually runs about an hour.

Do I have to buy anything?

No. There's no obligation and no pressure — a demo is about the food. If you decide later that a Thermomix® is right for you, I'm here to help.

Can we do this if I don't live in Wisconsin?

Yes. I run live demos over video for guests anywhere in the US. In-person demos are for the Door County, Wisconsin area.

Which model is available?

The current flagship is the Thermomix® TM7. I'll walk you through what it does and what comes with it when we meet.

How do I actually order one?

Orders go through the official Thermomix® shop. Vorwerk handles the payment, shipping and warranty directly; choosing me as your Consultant at checkout is what connects us.
