Meet Karen

I discovered the Thermomix® while living in Germany, and it made me fall back in love with cooking at home — with functionality I never knew a kitchen could have.

As a busy mother of three, I felt firsthand how it takes the stress out of getting dinner on the table and cuts down on grocery waste. If I'd had one when my kids were little, it would have changed everything.

I've been an Independent Thermomix® Consultant for over three years, in Germany and now here in the US. I'm local to Door County, Wisconsin, and I offer both in-person kitchen demos and live virtual sessions nationwide.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens in a Cooking Experience?

We cook a dish or two together — in your kitchen or over video — so you can see, taste, and ask questions. It's relaxed and hands-on, and it usually runs about an hour.

Do I have to buy anything during a demo?

No. There's no obligation and no pressure. A Cooking Experience is purely educational — if you decide the Thermomix® is right for you later, I'm here to help.

Can I do a demo if I don't live in Wisconsin?

Yes. I run live virtual sessions over video for guests anywhere in the US. In-person demos are for the Door County, Wisconsin area.

Which Thermomix® model is available?

The current flagship is the Thermomix® TM7. I'll walk you through what it does and what's included when you book a session.
